Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Resurrect Media ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, submit a form, or communicate with us by phone, text, or email.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a form, apply through an ad, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Business name and details you provide (e.g. monthly revenue range, service type)
  • Messages and call recordings/notes from communications with us
  • Technical data such as IP address, device/browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically via cookies and tracking pixels

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a strategy call
  • To send you SMS and email follow-up related to your inquiry, consistent with the consent you provide when submitting a form
  • To evaluate whether our services are a fit for your business
  • To improve our advertising performance and measure campaign results
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. SMS & Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in via a form, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Resurrect Media related to your inquiry and our services. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message, or by contacting us directly. Opting out of SMS does not affect any other services you've engaged us for.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Advertising & Tracking Technologies

We use tracking tools to measure the performance of our advertising, including:

  • Meta Pixel & Conversions API — to measure ad performance on Facebook and Instagram and improve ad targeting
  • Google Ads tracking (where applicable) — to measure ad performance on Google
  • GoHighLevel (GHL) — our CRM and automation platform, used to store contact and lead information and deliver follow-up communications

These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (e.g. GoHighLevel, Meta, Google, Signal House/A2P registration providers), solely to perform services on our behalf
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see Section 8).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain communications. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights regarding your data, contact us at the email or phone number listed on our site, or reply to any text/email you've received from us.

Note: This policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. Resurrect Media is not a law firm. If you have specific legal questions about privacy compliance, consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.
